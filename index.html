Questions

Before you grab it

Do I need to be online to use it?

No. PaceDeck is a single file that runs entirely in your browser. Open it once and it works with no internet connection from then on.

Is my athletes' data safe?

Everything stays in your own browser, on your own device. There's no server behind PaceDeck, so there's nothing to breach and nothing to leak.

Does it work on my phone or tablet, not just a laptop?

Yes — any modern browser on any device. No app store, no install.

What if I lose my data?

Built-in backup and restore lets you export a file and reload it on this device or a new one, any time.

Is this tied to one specific coaching system by name?

PaceDeck is built around the classic 7-zone color-coded pacing approach used widely in swim coaching — it's an independent tool, not an official product of any individual coach or program.

Why is it free for Patreon supporters but not everyone?

It's a thank-you for people already supporting ongoing development. If you'd rather pay once than join anything, the £9.99 one-time purchase gets you the exact same app.
